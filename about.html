About

Hi! I'm David. My dogs are my coworkers.

Web Design CNY is me and my two dogs, working out of West Utica. I make websites and graphics for the small shops and side hustles we walk past every day.

West Utica HQ
Why I do this

A neighbor, not an agency

Utica is full of great small businesses: family delis, barbershops, salons, bakeries, markets and auto shops run by people from all over the world. A lot of them have a Facebook page or nothing at all, and no website button on their Google listing.

Agencies charge agency prices. I keep it simple: a fair flat price, a site you own, and a real person nearby you can reach when something needs changing.

And the dogs? They supervise. Every website gets their approval before it goes live.
