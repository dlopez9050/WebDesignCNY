Questions owners ask

Straight answers

Something else on your mind? Email me.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay once. The only other cost is your web address, about $10–20 a year, paid to the company that registers it, in your name.

What if I don't like the free sample?

Then you owe nothing. The sample is free and there's no contract. You only pay once you decide to go ahead.

Who owns the site?

You do. The web address is registered in your name and your accounts stay in your name. I only get manager access to help.

I already have a Facebook page.

Keep it. Your site links to it. A website shows up on Google for people who don't use Facebook, and it gives your Google listing a website button.

What do you need from me?

Your menu or list of services, prices, a few photos and how you take orders or bookings. Photos from your phone are fine.

How long does it take?

The free sample usually takes a couple of days. The full site is usually live about a week after I get your information, depending on how quickly you can review it.

Can I change things later?

Yes. Send me what to change. Small fixes in the first month are free; after that I'll tell you the price before I do anything.

Do you print flyers and cards?

I design them and send you print-ready files. You take them to any print shop and pay them directly, so you're never paying a markup.

Do you work outside Utica?

Yes. Most of the work happens by email and text, so anywhere in Central New York works: Rome, New Hartford, Whitesboro, Yorkville, New York Mills and beyond.
