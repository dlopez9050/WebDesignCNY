Who's building it

A neighbor, not an agency

I live in West Utica and I walk past a lot of great local businesses that people can't find online. A lot of them have a Facebook page or nothing at all, and no website button on their Google listing.

I keep it simple: a fair flat price, a site you own, and a real person you can reach when something needs changing.

Questions owners ask

Straight answers

Is there a monthly fee?

No. You pay $200 once. The only other cost is your web address, about $10–20 a year, paid to the company that registers it.

Who owns the site?

You do. The web address is registered in your name, so it stays yours no matter what.

I already have a Facebook page.

Keep it. Your site links to it. A website shows up on Google for people who don't use Facebook, and it gives your Google listing a website button.

What do you need from me?

Your menu or list of services, prices, a few photos and how you take orders or bookings. Photos from your phone are fine.

How long does it take?

Usually about a week from when I get your information, depending on how quickly you can review it.

Can I change things later?

Yes. Send me what to change. Small fixes in the first month are free; after that I'll tell you the price before I do anything.

Want to see yours?

Email me your business name. I'll make a free sample homepage and send it to you, usually within a couple of days.
